    The saved model re-run over the last 30 days of each buoy's live file, using only readings up to each forecast time. These days are after the model's training period.     How reliable is it?

    Scores for the test period, July to December 2024: forecasts the model never saw during training. Skill is how much smaller the average error is than the "waves stay as they are" guess (persistence). The range is a 95% range from resampling days.

    Quality checks

    Against NOAA's wave model

    The same buoys and the same forecast times, scored against what the buoy measured. GFS-Wave is NOAA's operational wave model (WAVEWATCH III driven by the GFS weather forecast). It covers four weeks of October 2024 and three buoys, so treat it as a small check, not a ranking. Average miss in metres (lower is better).

    Data: NOAA National Data Buoy Center (NDBC) standard meteorological files, including the live realtime files and the Canadian buoys (Environment and Climate Change Canada) that NDBC serves, and surface drifters from NOAA's Global Drifter Program, and GFS-Wave point forecasts from NOAA's open data on AWS.
